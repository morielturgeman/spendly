Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app

We do not collect payment information. Spendly is a free tracking tool and does not process any financial transactions.

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service:

  • To display your expense history and summaries
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns

We will never use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service you signed up for.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls that restrict who can view your data.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use the following limited third-party services to operate the app:

  • Hosting providers to serve the application
  • Error monitoring tools to detect and fix bugs

These services are bound by their own privacy policies and are only granted access to the minimum data necessary to perform their function.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also export your expense data from your account settings. If you have concerns about how your data is handled, you may contact us directly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is used, please contact us at support@spendly.local
